QUICK FACTS ABOUT ELEPHANT

7 MINUTES

As a keystone species, bush elephants shape entire ecosystems across the African continent—clearing dense brush, propagating native acacia seeds across vast migrations, and excavating deep seasonal water trails that nourish hundreds of companion species through dry savanna cycles.

Habitat Woodland and savanna
Scientific name Loxodonta africana
Adult weight 2,000 – 6,300 kg
IUCN status Vulnerable
